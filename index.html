How it works

Floor, phone, rival. Go.

No equipment and no setup. A match takes about as long as it takes to read this page.

Before

Set your phone down

Lean it against something low on the floor, facing you. The camera finds your head, shoulders and hands, then counts you in from ten.

60 seconds

Push

Your rival's count climbs beside yours while you go. Only full reps score. Half reps don't.

After

Take the points

Win and you climb the ladder. Lose and you see exactly how many reps it would have taken.

The app

More than a match.

Ranked

A real person, right now.

Find a match and the search looks for someone on camera at the same moment. Nobody there within 15 seconds, and you race a set another lifter recorded earlier, rep for rep, for the same rating. There is always someone to beat.

  • Both counts climb side by side, live.
  • Giving up mid-round is a loss.
  • Your first match places you at Iron V.
The referee

Only full reps.

The bar beside you is the referee. It empties as you go down, turns green at the bottom, and fills back up as you push. Full again, and the rep counts.

0 Go downGo up

If your shape breaks, the bar turns orange and that rep doesn't count. The number you watch climb is the number that's scored.

Friends

Race your friends live.

Add friends by username and invite one to a live match. They get a minute to join, wherever they are. Friend matches are friendly: no rank on the line, just the head-to-head record. A board of just your friends ranks you all by rating, with the score between you.

Boards

Top your country.

Weekly and monthly boards for your country and for the whole world. See where you stand, and who is just above you.

Challenge

Dare anyone with a link.

After a match, send your minute as a link. Whoever opens it races your count at the pace you really did it, one try each, for seven days. You see everybody who took it, and who beat you.

Your lifter

Make the lifter that stands for you.

Pick the face, hair, beard and kit of the lifter who stands in for you on every board, in every match and on your profile, or use a photo. Some parts are earned. Pro adds more.

Beat your past self

Race last week’s you.

Start a six-week plan from your best minute. Pick a goal and three to five days a week of short sets the camera counts. Every Sunday you race your own best minute from the week before, at the pace you really did it, and watch your line climb towards the goal.

  • Free for everyone.
  • A goal a quarter, a half or three quarters above your best.
  • Friendly: no rank on the line.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need any equipment?

Just your phone and some floor. Lean the phone against something low, facing you, so the camera can see your head, shoulders and hands.

Does any video leave my phone?

No. Reps are counted on your phone, and the camera picture is never sent anywhere. In a live match the only thing your phone sends the other player is your rep count.

What if nobody is online?

If nobody is found live within 15 seconds, you race a set another lifter recorded earlier, rep for rep. There is always someone to beat.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. OUTREPD is free, with an ad when you leave the result of a game. You can also choose to watch a short video for a small reward: a match’s XP twice, a streak back, or a Pro look for a day. OUTREPD Pro, weekly or yearly, removes the ads and adds Pro looks for your lifter; it is billed by the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time in your store account.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Invite a friend to a live match and they have a minute to join. Friend matches don't move your rank.

Can I challenge someone who doesn’t have the app?

Yes. After a match, send your minute as a challenge link. Whoever opens it sees your count, and races it in the app at the pace you really did it. One try each, open for seven days, and no rank on the line.

Who can play?

Anyone 13 or over, on iPhone or Android. Sign in with Apple or Google.

What we can help with

  • Subscription questions — billing, cancellations, or plan changes
  • Bug reports — something not working as expected
  • Feedback — ideas, suggestions, or feature requests
  • Data requests — export or delete your data
  • Reports — reporting objectionable content or abusive behaviour

Account deletion

To delete your account, go to Settings → Data → Delete account inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, email support@outrepd.com from the email address on your account (or include your username) with the subject "Delete my outrepd account". We will process your request within 30 days.

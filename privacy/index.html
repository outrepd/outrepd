Privacy Policy

outrepd · Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what the outrepd app ("outrepd", "we", "us") collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to get a copy or delete it. outrepd is run by MK Labs. Questions go to support@outrepd.com.

outrepd is a push-up game: your phone's camera counts your reps, and you race other people — live, both on camera at the same time, or against a set they recorded earlier.

The camera never leaves your phone

The camera is used only to count reps. Pose detection runs on your device. No video, image or body-position data from the camera is sent to us or to anyone else.

In a live match, the only thing your phone sends the other player's phone while the round runs is your rep count — a number. Never video, pictures or body positions.

To check the counter's accuracy, the app may keep a recording of body positions (points on a skeleton, not video) on your phone. It stays on the phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Pose detection uses Google's ML Kit library, which runs on the device. Google describes what ML Kit itself may send to Google in its terms: developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.

What it writes there never reaches us, is never used for advertising and is never shared. You can turn it off in Settings, and remove what was written in the Health app or Health Connect.

How we use it

To run outrepd: matches, rankings, Adventure, friends, notifications, your profile, keeping the game fair, and answering you when you contact us. And to show ads to accounts without Pro, as described under Ads. Apart from those ads, we do not use your data for advertising, we do not build profiles of you for anyone else, and we do not use analytics tools. Crash reports are used only to fix the app.

Ads

outrepd is free to use and shows an ad when you leave the result of a game. There are no ads in your first three games, and none at all with OUTREPD Pro. The ads come from Google AdMob and are limited to content rated for teens.

To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google receives information from your phone: your IP address, device and app information, how you interact with the ad, and your phone's advertising identifier where it is available. Google's policies cover what it does with it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On iPhone, the app asks before your advertising identifier can be used to track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, it is not shared, and the ads are not personalised with it. You can change your answer in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you before the first ad, and you can refuse personalised ads there.

What other people can see

Other players can see your username, photo, rank, record, best set, streak, the title you wear, your leaderboard places and your month awards. You can hide yourself from the leaderboards, and hide your country, in Settings → Privacy. Anyone with your share link can see your share card without signing in. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Apart from the ads described under Ads, we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers:

  • Supabase hosts the app's server, database and profile photos, and relays the rep counts between the two phones in a live match.
  • Resend sends the email with your data when you ask for a copy.
  • RevenueCat manages the OUTREPD Pro subscription. It receives your account ID and your purchase records from Google Play or the App Store, and tells the app and our server whether your subscription is active. Its privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications to your phone (Firebase Cloud Messaging — on iPhone, through Apple's push notification service) and collects crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). Its privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads to accounts without Pro, as described under Ads. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect players from fraud or abuse. Your data may be processed in countries other than yours.

How we protect it

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our hosting provider encrypts stored data. Database rules let each account read and change only what it is allowed to, and rank and results can only be changed by the server, not by the app on your phone.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed from our database straight away (see Deleting your account). Copies in routine backups are removed as those backups expire. Reports other players filed about you are kept after your account is deleted, with your account removed from them, so abuse can still be dealt with.

Your choices and rights

Get a copy of your data: Settings → Data → Export my data. It is emailed to you.

Change what others see: Settings → Privacy.

Correct your data: change your username from your profile once every 14 days, and your photo and settings at any time. For anything else, email us.

Delete your data: delete your account, below.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict processing, or to complain to a data protection authority. Email support@outrepd.com and we will answer within 30 days. We process your data to provide the service you asked for, and the ads on the basis described under Ads. We do not sell personal information. Some US state laws count personalised ads as "sharing" personal information; you can stop it with your phone's ad and tracking settings described under Ads, or with Pro, which removes the ads.

Deleting your account

In the app: open outrepd, go to Settings → Data → Delete account, and confirm.

Without the app: email support@outrepd.com from the email address on your account, or tell us your username, with the subject "Delete my outrepd account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your account, email address, username, profile photo, sets, matches, live matches and invites, Adventure progress, titles and streak, month awards, friends, blocks, mutes, reports you filed, notifications, push notification addresses and settings.

What is kept: opponents keep the rank points they won or lost against you, because those are part of their own records, but the match is removed from their history. Reports other players filed about you are kept with your account removed from them.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because Google Play or the App Store bills it, not us. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Deletion cannot be undone.

Children

outrepd is only for people aged 13 and over, and the app turns away anyone who says they are younger. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
